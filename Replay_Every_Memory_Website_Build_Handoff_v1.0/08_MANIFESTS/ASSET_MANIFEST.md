# Asset manifest guide

The machine-readable inventory is `asset-manifest.json`.

## Approved production masters

- Primary horizontal lockup
- Compact icon
- Navy and gold separated wordmarks
- Tagline/rule lockup
- Trashy running silhouette

The Trashy identity reference and low-resolution approved direction image are included for visual verification. They are not default public media.

## Reference-only images

Eight recovered inspiration images are included in `04_BRAND_ASSETS/reference_only/`. Their rights, ownership, and production use are not established. They must not enter public output, derivative assets, or generated training/reference pipelines without a separate rights and approval decision.

## Derivative policy

Production may create responsive size/format derivatives of approved masters. Record each derivative's source master, dimensions, encoder/settings, intended placement, and checksum. Do not alter composition, color, proportions, or silhouette.
