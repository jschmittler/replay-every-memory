## Overview

# A very bad kind of trash.

Trashy is a curious raccoon with a talent for finding value in things other creatures throw away.

One morning, he and his best friend Jimothy discover a strange purple-black substance spreading through their forest.

The Gunk attracts nearby animals, mutates them, and can take control without completely erasing the personality trapped underneath.

Knocking the infected creatures down does not cure them.

So Trashy and Jimothy follow the contamination beyond home.

Their search takes them from the forest into suburbia and the city, where the Gunk grows thicker, its creatures become more coordinated, and the evidence increasingly suggests that the outbreak is not an accident.

To save their friends, they have to find the source.

---

## Core Experience

### Clear, approachable platforming

The goal should make sense before the game starts asking more of you.

*Trash Dash* is being built around readable movement, clear feedback, expressive animation, and challenges that teach through play.

### Characters with something to say before they speak

Trashy, Jimothy, friends, enemies, and bosses are designed around recognizable silhouettes, readable poses, and personalities that stay intact while they move.

### A world that keeps getting bigger

The adventure begins in Trashy's forest home and follows the Gunk through new places, new threats, and increasingly strange evidence about where it came from.

### Old-school clarity, modern craft

*Trash Dash* learns from the immediacy and readability of classic platformers without trying to recreate one particular generation of games.

---

## Trashy

# Trashy

Trashy is curious, resourceful, a little mischievous, and extremely confident in his ability to identify worthwhile garbage.

He collects forgotten things because he believes almost anything might become useful eventually.

That turns out to be a much more important skill than expected.

---

## Jimothy

# Jimothy

Jimothy is Trashy's best friend, neighbor, and a raccoon with a dangerous amount of confidence around buttons.

Where Trashy hesitates, Jimothy usually jumps.

Where Trashy asks why, Jimothy has often already pressed it.

He is chaotic, loyal, surprisingly useful, and absolutely coming with you whether or not anyone asked.

---

## The Gunk

# The Gunk

The Gunk is a thick purple-black substance spreading through the world.

Animals are strangely attracted to it.

Once infected, they mutate and become aggressive, but defeating them does not undo what happened.

Trashy slowly realizes that the creatures are not simply monsters.

They are victims.

And something is controlling them.

## Why We Exist

# To make games that become part of people's lives.

The best games do not disappear when the screen turns off.

We remember where we were.

The level we finally beat.

The music.

Replay Every Memory exists to make new games capable of creating those kinds of memories.

By learning from it.

## The Origin

# Games were already part of the family.

Games have been part of James's life across generations of hardware, genres, interfaces, and technology.

They are also how James and Emily met.

They are something James and Emily now share with Matilda and Ramona.

Eventually the conversation changed from playing games to making one.

*Trash Dash* began as an experiment and grew into a world, a cast, a design system, a family project, and a real game.

Replay Every Memory grew around it.

## The Name

# Replay. Every. Memory.

### Replay

Start again.

Return to a favorite world.

Learn from another attempt.

Hand an old favorite to someone who has never played it before.

### Every

Every generation of games.

Every kind of player.

Every age at which play can matter.

Every new memory that has not happened yet.

### Memory

What stays after the game ends.

The person beside you.

The music you still remember.

The level you finally beat.

And, in game language, the systems that store progress so you can come back later.

### REM

REM is rapid eye movement, the stage of sleep associated with vivid dreaming.

It is also Ramona, Emily, and Matilda.

The family at the center of the studio's story.

The full public name is always Replay Every Memory.

## Our Approach

# Old-school clarity. Modern craft.

We care about:

- Clear goals
- Responsive interaction
- Strong silhouettes
- Readable animation
- Expressive characters
- Clever level design
- Useful feedback
- Humor with a reason to be there
- Technology that improves the experience
- Revising the work until it feels right

Modern tools, including AI, can help us explore, prototype, build, test, and improve.

Human judgment stays at the center.

The point is not to remove the work that gives a game personality.

It is to make more room for it.
