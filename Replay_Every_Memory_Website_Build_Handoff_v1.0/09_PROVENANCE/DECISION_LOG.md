# Decision log

## Brand and studio

- Replay Every Memory is the full public studio name.
- REM remains a supporting monogram.
- “Dreams with a start button.” is the exact tagline.
- Trashy is the mascot and the running silhouette/head system anchors the identity.
- The approved logo direction is represented by the supplied full-color high-resolution masters.
- Bright Pixel Theory material is superseded.

## Website strategy and structure

- The website leads with *Trash Dash*, then development evidence, then the fuller studio story.
- The Playable Memory direction is approved: expressive and game-aware, but clear and useful rather than a novelty game interface.
- Primary navigation: Trash Dash, Journal, Studio, Press.
- Contact, Accessibility, and Privacy remain utility destinations.
- The current primary action is Follow Development, routed to the Journal until a real external destination exists.
- The Journal is a core studio product organized around actual problems and decisions.
- Desktop reference width is 1440 px; mobile reference width is 390 px.

## Content lock

- *Trash Dash* is in development and planned for 2026 on Steam, App Store, and Google Play.
- RC1 is in beta testing.
- Trailer, demo, and storefront pages are coming soon.
- Story material is divided into public-now, planned-reveal, and locked-spoiler tiers.
- The only approved public roster is James, Emily, Ramona, and Matilda.
- Fictional provisional contributors are superseded.
- Public email is trashy@replayeverymemory.com; handle is @replayeverymemory.
- WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the design/development target, not a conformance claim.
- Privacy-minimizing measurement is the principle; provider choice remains open.
- Contact provider choice remains open; direct email is the approved fallback.

## Legal presentation

- Do not publish an LLC suffix before formation/name confirmation.
- Do not use ® without confirmed registration.
- Footer: © 2026 Replay Every Memory. All rights reserved.
- Optional ™ use remains a presentation decision rather than a required lock.
