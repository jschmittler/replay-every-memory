# Replay Every Memory — approved brand foundation

## Locked identity

- Public name: **Replay Every Memory**
- Supporting monogram: **REM**
- Tagline: **Dreams with a start button.**
- Descriptor: **Independent Game Studio**
- First title: ***Trash Dash***
- Mascot and north-star character: **Trashy**

The full name should lead in public-facing contexts. REM is a compact supporting mark, not a replacement studio name. The letters also carry the studio's dual meaning: rapid eye movement and Ramona, Emily, and Matilda.

## Studio idea

Replay Every Memory is a homegrown independent game studio built from a lifetime of play. It exists to make games capable of becoming part of people's lives: memories tied to characters, music, challenge, humor, connection, and the people who were there.

The studio does not recreate the past. It studies why memorable games stayed with people and uses modern tools and judgment to make the next memory.

## Brand promise

**Easy to start. Worth continuing.**

The work should offer a clear way in, reward continued attention, make systems readable, and let character and craft carry the memory.

## Principles

1. **Play across generations.** Give new players a clear way in without exhausting what experienced players can discover.
2. **Old-school clarity, modern craft.** Learn from older games without imitating one generation's surface style.
3. **Human scale, real craft.** Being homegrown keeps decisions close to the work; it does not lower the standard.
4. **Easy to start, worth continuing.** The opening should make sense and the experience should give people a reason to stay.
5. **Characters carry the memory.** Silhouette, movement, expression, voice, reactions, mistakes, and victories matter.

## Voice

- Candid, warm, observant, playful, and specific.
- Plain language before marketing language.
- Confident about decisions; honest about work in progress.
- Humor comes from character, situation, and the occasional raccoon problem—not from trying to sound quirky.
- Explain what was made, what failed, what changed, and why.

Avoid:

- Corporate scale theater.
- Empty superlatives such as revolutionary, epic, groundbreaking, or unforgettable.
- Nostalgia as costume.
- Lore dumps and spoiler-heavy teasing.
- Unsupported feature claims.
- Fictional staff, invented dates, invented partners, or invented metrics.

## Website role

The website is the first complete public expression of the brand. It must quickly establish:

1. What Replay Every Memory is.
2. What *Trash Dash* is.
3. Why the studio exists.
4. What the visitor can do now.
5. How the work is being made.

Lead with the game. Show evidence. Reveal the fuller studio story after the work is understandable.
