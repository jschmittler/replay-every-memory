## Public story premise

Approved synopsis:

> When a strange purple-black substance called the Gunk begins corrupting the animals around his forest home, Trashy and his best friend Jimothy follow the contamination beyond the woods in search of its source and a way to save the creatures caught in it.

Public-safe facts:

- Trashy begins as an ordinary, curious raccoon with a love of forgotten things and strong opinions about good trash.
- Jimothy is Trashy's unpredictable, loyal best friend and neighbor.
- The Gunk is a thick purple-black contamination.
- Animals are drawn to it, mutate, become aggressive, and retain something of themselves underneath the corruption.
- Defeating an infected creature does not cure it.
- Trashy and Jimothy follow the contamination from the forest through suburbia and into the city.
- The evidence increasingly suggests the outbreak is not accidental.
