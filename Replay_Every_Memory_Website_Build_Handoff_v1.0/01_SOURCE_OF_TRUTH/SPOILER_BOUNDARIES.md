# Trash Dash public reveal and spoiler boundaries

The full story in `05_STORY_SOURCE/trash-dash-full-story.md` is an internal reference. It is not public website copy.

## Tier 1 — public now

Safe across the website, metadata, press boilerplate, and Journal references:

- Trashy and Jimothy.
- Trashy's ordinary forest life and search for breakfast.
- The Gunk's purple-black appearance and corrupting effect.
- Infected animals are victims and are not cured by defeat.
- Forest / Woodlands, suburbia, and the big city.
- The journey follows the contamination toward its source.
- The outbreak appears increasingly deliberate.

## Tier 2 — hold for a planned reveal

Do not publish until the studio approves the corresponding marketing beat:

- The research facility or tower.
- Project O.P.O.S.S.U.M.
- Pizza Rat King.
- The secret space center, rocket, and travel into space.
- Specific boss identities beyond what is separately revealed in approved media.

## Tier 3 — locked spoilers

Do not publish in page copy, metadata, structured data, filenames, alt text, source comments, tests, sample CMS content, or hidden DOM:

- Garbagia.
- Galactogobbler.
- The possible extraterrestrial origin of raccoons.
- The Gunk cure/resolution and hive-mind outcome.
- The destruction/defeat resolution.
- The time-travel pod.
- The future abandoned baseball stadium.
- The ending and sequel setup.

## Implementation rule

Search the production output—not only source files—for Tier 2 and Tier 3 terms before shipping. Internal source materials must never be copied into a public static directory or bundled client-side.
