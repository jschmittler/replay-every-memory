# Replay Every Memory Website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated for the production build: preserve the target repository's existing supported stack. If the target is empty, use a static-first, accessible TypeScript stack suitable for content-heavy marketing pages and a development journal; the build prompt recommends Astro but does not make the choice a public brand fact.

## Users

- Players and families deciding whether to follow *Trash Dash*.
- Press, creators, partners, and storefront reviewers looking for accurate studio and game information.
- Visitors who need contact, accessibility, privacy, or downloadable press materials.

## Product Purpose

Present Replay Every Memory as the homegrown independent game studio behind *Trash Dash*, make the game immediately understandable, document its development candidly, and give visitors a useful next action while trailer, demo, and store pages remain unavailable.

## Positioning

The site leads with the work, then reveals the family studio and its purpose. Its Journal is a primary product surface that shows problems, experiments, revisions, and decisions instead of functioning as a generic news feed.

## Operating Context

The launch site must support Home, Trash Dash, Journal index, Journal article, Studio, Press, Contact, Accessibility, Privacy, a mobile navigation state, and a useful 404. The content model must later support a second game without structural replacement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary navigation: Trash Dash, Journal, Studio, Press.
- Current primary action: Follow Development, routed to the Journal until a confirmed follow mechanism or storefront exists.
- *Trash Dash* is in development, planned for 2026 on Steam, the App Store, and Google Play.
- Trailer, demo, and storefront links are unresolved and must remain honest coming-soon states.
- Public story content must remain inside the documented spoiler-safe boundary.
- Legal entity, trademark registration, hosting, CMS, analytics, form provider, final URLs, and production technology are unresolved.

## Brand Commitments

- Public studio name: Replay Every Memory.
- Supporting monogram: REM.
- Tagline: Dreams with a start button.
- Descriptor: Independent Game Studio.
- First title: *Trash Dash*.
- Mascot/identity anchor: Trashy, especially the approved running silhouette and head/icon system.
- Public handle: @replayeverymemory.
- Public email: trashy@replayeverymemory.com.
- The superseded Bright Pixel Theory identity and fictional creator roster must never appear.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved brand and factual locks in `01_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/`.
- Final page copy in `02_WEBSITE_COPY/`.
- Responsive structural specifications and a dependency-free review prototype in `03_WIREFRAMES/`.
- Approved Replay Every Memory logo and Trashy identity assets in `04_BRAND_ASSETS/approved/`.
- Full internal narrative and public reveal boundaries in `05_STORY_SOURCE/`.
- No approved trailer, gameplay capture set, storefront URLs, social URLs, legal registration record, analytics vendor, form vendor, or CMS proof is included.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the game and prove the work before telling the full studio story.
- Make the experience easy to start and worth continuing.
- Use old-school clarity with modern craft, not retro imitation.
- Keep a human scale without lowering the production standard.
- Preserve facts, spoiler boundaries, and recognizable character identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA in design and development, but do not claim conformance before production testing. The site must work without motion, sound, hover, or color alone; remain keyboard operable; respect reduced-motion preferences; use semantic structure and visible focus; and preserve readable game art and text at all supported widths.
